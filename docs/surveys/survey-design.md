# Survey Design — Bousla

## Student survey
Explores passing courses without understanding concepts, prerequisite gaps, timing of discovering misunderstandings, help-seeking behavior and barriers, course connections, career skills, perceived usefulness of knowledge maps and learning paths, and privacy concerns.

## Faculty survey
Explores methods for assessing understanding during lectures, frequency of discovering misunderstandings after exams, perceived usefulness of aggregate classroom insights, willingness to use short questions, and suggestions or concerns.

Do not report findings before responses are collected and analyzed. Store only aggregate, non-identifying summaries in the repository. Do not commit raw respondent data.
